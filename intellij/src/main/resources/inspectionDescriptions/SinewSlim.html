Reports a method at or over the length cap (26 code lines by default; trigger in .sinew-slim.yml) with SinewSlim's verdict: slimmable at its cheapest seams, slimmable only by returning a record, slimmable but every piece bound to the class, or not slimmable and to be investigated for redesign.

The quick fix, the gutter mark and Slim this method show the proposal over the method, each piece with an Extract… button that asks only for the new method's name.
